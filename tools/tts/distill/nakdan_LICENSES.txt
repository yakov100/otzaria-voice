Otzaria small diacritizer — third-party notices
===============================================

This pack contains:

  nakdan.onnx            A small diacritizer (adds nikud to Hebrew text): a
                         character BiLSTM of 4.25M parameters, int8 ONNX,
                         trained by the Otzaria project
                         (tools/tts/distill/nakdan_tiny/).
  nakdan_tokenizer.json  DictaBERT's character tokenizer, unchanged.
  nikud_lexicon.bin      How words of Talmudic Hebrew and Aramaic are
                         vocalized (tools/tts/build_nikud_lexicon.py).


1. How nakdan.onnx was made
---------------------------

It was trained to reproduce the vocalization of:

  * The vocalized Babylonian Talmud of Dicta: The Israel Center for Text
    Analysis — https://github.com/Dicta-Israel-Center-for-Text-Analysis/Talmud-Bavli-with-Nikud
    License: Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike (CC BY-SA).
    Credit, as the project asks: "Vocalized Talmud text by Dicta, The Israel
    Center for Text Analysis" / "ניקוד התלמוד: דיקטה - המרכז הישראלי לניתוח
    טקסטים".
  * Vocalized books of the Otzaria library whose text is in the public
    domain or under CC BY / CC BY-SA.
  * Unvocalized books of the library, vocalized for training by the Phonikud
    diacritizer (MIT, https://github.com/thewh1teagle/phonikud — the Phonikud
    project asks that attribution include this link) together with the
    lexicon below.

As a derivative of CC BY-SA material, nakdan.onnx is offered under CC BY-SA
4.0: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/

3. nikud_lexicon.bin
--------------------

Built by the Otzaria project by counting how each word is vocalized in the
vocalized Talmud of Dicta (CC BY-SA, credit as above) and in vocalized books
of the Otzaria library (public domain, CC BY or CC BY-SA). It holds no text,
only words and their marks, and is offered under CC BY-SA 4.0.
