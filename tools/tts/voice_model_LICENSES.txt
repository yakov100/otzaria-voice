Otzaria voice model (reading aloud) — third-party notices
========================================================

This pack contains:

  nakdan.onnx, nakdan_tokenizer.json
         Phonikud diacritizer (adds nikud to Hebrew text), int8 ONNX.
  text_tokenizer.json, embed_tokens.onnx(_data), language_model.onnx,
  language_model_q4.onnx_data
         Chatterbox Multilingual text-to-speech, ONNX export; the language
         model with 4-bit weights.
  decoder.onnx, conditional_decoder_quantized.onnx_data
         The one-step speech decoder of Chatterbox Turbo, ONNX, int8.
  voices/chaim.otzvoice
         The voice "חיים" (Chaim): speaker conditioning derived by the
         Otzaria project from a recording of its contributor.

2. Phonikud diacritizer (nakdan.onnx)
-------------------------------------

Source: https://huggingface.co/thewh1teagle/phonikud-onnx
        (file phonikud-1.0.int8.onnx)
Project: Phonikud — https://github.com/thewh1teagle/phonikud
License: MIT (model card of phonikud-onnx).

The Phonikud project asks that attribution include a clear link to it:
https://github.com/thewh1teagle/phonikud

The service uses only the nikud and shin/sin outputs; Phonikud's stress,
vocal-shva and prefix marks are not used.


3. DictaBERT-large-char-menaked (Dicta) — base of the diacritizer
------------------------------------------------------------------

Source: https://huggingface.co/dicta-il/dictabert-large-char-menaked
Author: Dicta: The Israel Center for Text Analysis
License: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0)
         https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

nakdan_tokenizer.json is DictaBERT's character tokenizer, unchanged.
The diacritizer in nakdan.onnx is Phonikud's fine-tuned version of this
model (see section 2), not Dicta's own release.


4. The voice "חיים" (voices/chaim.otzvoice)
--------------------------------------------

Derived by the Otzaria project, with the speech encoder of Chatterbox
Multilingual, from a recording of the project's contributor, who agreed to
its use for reading text aloud with this service. It holds no audio, only
the speaker conditioning the models need.
